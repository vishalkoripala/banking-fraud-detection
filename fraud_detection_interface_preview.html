The Problem

Finding 2 fakes hiding in every 1,000 purchases

Picture a huge pile of card purchases. Almost every single one is a real person buying something real. But every so often, one purchase is someone using a stolen card. Our job is to catch those — without ever accusing an honest shopper.

284,807
real purchases we studied
492
were actually stolen cards
1 in 580
how rare a fake one is

See that tiny red line on the end? That's every single fake purchase, drawn next to all the honest ones. If our guard got lazy and said "everyone's honest, always" — it would be right 998 times out of 1,000. It would also never catch a single thief. Being "right most of the time" isn't the same as actually doing the job.

How Well Does It Work?

We tested it on 56,962 purchases it had never seen

Before trusting any guard, you'd want to see it tested on cases it doesn't already know the answer to. So that's exactly what we did.

🔔 Think of a smoke alarm. A good one catches real fires and doesn't go off every time you make toast. Our guard has to do both jobs at once — catch real thieves, and leave honest shoppers alone.
81%
Out of 98 real stolen-card purchases in the test, our guard caught 79 of them.
99.97%
Out of 56,864 honest purchases, it wrongly blamed only 16 of them.
